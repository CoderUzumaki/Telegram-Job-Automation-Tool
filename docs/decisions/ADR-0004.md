# ADR-0004: Local-first retention and provenance

Status: Recorded specification baseline; implementation verification pending
Owner: Project maintainer
Date: 2026-10-23
Reference: JR-002; LLD §§6,10,13; D-001–003

## Context

Preserve the approved MVP boundaries while allowing requirements and adapters to change independently.

## Decision

Retain recognized structured jobs regardless of match outcome. Configure raw-text TTL separately from history import; baseline choices are no retention, 7 days, 30 days and until deletion, with proposed 7-day default. Keep policy in services, not entity constants. SourceMessage, JobObservation and canonical Job remain distinct with field provenance. Explicit erasure overrides historical retention; raw expiry preserves structured facts.

## Consequences and alternatives

Local storage is not permission to archive indefinitely. Source deletion must remove exclusive facts without deleting independently supported cross-post facts. No cloud sync or telemetry by default.

## Verification

I08/I09 prove provenance-aware erasure and raw expiry. Review backups, indexes and diagnostics for erased-data resurrection.

## Supersession

No replacement. Material changes require a new ADR and corresponding contract/test updates.
